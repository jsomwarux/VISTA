# VISTA Support

## About VISTA

VISTA is a social movie rating app that lets you rate films on a 1-100 scale, share your opinions with friends, and discover how your taste compares to others. Track what you watch, build your cinematic profile, and find your film community.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I rate a movie?

Tap the floating "+" button on any screen to open the rating modal. Search for a movie, adjust the score slider (1-100), optionally write a review, and tap "Save Rating." You can update your rating anytime by rating the same movie again.

### What is the Taste Match score?

The Taste Match percentage shows how similarly you and another user rate the same movies. A higher percentage means you tend to agree on film quality. You need at least 3 movies in common for a score to appear.

### How do I find friends?

Go to the Friends tab and use the search bar to find users by username or display name. Tap on a user to view their profile, then tap "Follow" to add them to your feed.

### Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your profile, ratings, and all associated data within 30 days.

### Why can't I see someone's Taste Match score?

The Taste Match score requires at least 3 movies that both you and the other user have rated. Keep rating movies to unlock compatibility scores with more users.
